Services

Three disciplines that are usually split across three firms.

C

Construction

RIBA Stage 3 to 5

Taking a spatially coordinated design and turning it into a set of information a contractor can build from. We coordinate the architect, engineers and specialist designers so the drawings, specifications and schedules agree with each other before they reach site.

  • Stage 3 design coordination
  • Stage 4 technical design and specification
  • Stage 5 construction information and queries
  • Tender packages and contractor appointment
S

Surveying

Quantity Surveying

Cost control from first estimate to final account. Budgets built from measured quantities rather than allowances, valuations assessed against work actually done, and variations priced and agreed before they become disputes.

  • Cost plans and bills of quantities
  • Tender analysis and contract sum
  • Interim valuations and payment notices
  • Variations, claims and final account
M

Management

Construction Project Management

Running the build day to day. We sit between the client and the main contractor, hold the programme, chair progress meetings and keep sub-contractors, suppliers and budgets moving in the same direction.

  • Programme and progress reporting
  • Contractor and sub-contractor liaison
  • Contract administration under JCT
  • Practical completion and defects
How we work

Fewer handovers, fewer gaps.

Most residential projects lose money and time at the joints between consultants. When the person coordinating the design also measures it and then manages the build, there is no one to pass the problem to.

Written down

Instructions, costs and decisions are recorded as they happen. If it matters to the contract, it is in writing before it is on site.

Measured

Costs are built from quantities and rates, with the working shown. A total without the arithmetic behind it is an opinion.

Contract first

We work to the contract form the project is on, usually JCT, and advise on the clause rather than the general idea.

On site

Progress is checked against the programme by someone who has walked the floor, not read a report about it.

Straight answers

If a budget will not hold or a date will not be met, the client hears it from us first and early.
